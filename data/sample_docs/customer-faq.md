# Example Product FAQ

## Account access
If a user cannot sign in, ask them to confirm the email address and use the password reset flow. Never ask for a password or one-time verification code.

## Response times
Standard support requests are reviewed within one business day. Urgent service outages should be marked as high priority and escalated to the support lead.

## Billing questions
For billing discrepancies, collect the invoice number and a short description. Do not request full payment card details. Billing changes require review by an authorized team member.

## Escalation
Escalate security concerns, suspected account compromise, and unresolved service outages to the appropriate human team. These are fictional FAQ entries for the demo.
